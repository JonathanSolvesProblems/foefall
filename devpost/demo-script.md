# Foefall demo video: script

The event caps the video at one minute. About 130 words of narration, read at an easy pace, fits in 55 seconds.
Record the narration first, then capture each shot to fit it. Every shot is the real game: the Reddit app on the phone
for anything inside Reddit, Orvandel itself for the first shot.

| Time | Shot | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Orvandel: the Duskfield Wolf's attack marked on the ground before it lands (`EpicMMO/preview/03-first-fight-telegraph.jpg`, or a short clip of the same fight) | In Orvandel, the MMO I'm building, big attacks are marked on the ground before they land. |
| 0:05 | Cut to the r/Foefall feed: today's Foefall card with its date, moon and board | Foefall brings that to Reddit as a daily puzzle. |
| 0:09 | Tap Begin. The board opens with every red strike tile showing | Every monster shows exactly where it will strike. |
| 0:13 | Select the Hexweaver, swap it with the Thief, then shove the Wolf. The forecast counts up to every monster falling | My heroes can't win a fight. They move the monsters so the strikes land on each other, and the forecast shows who falls before I commit. |
| 0:24 | Press Strike: every monster strikes at once, all of them fall, the lamp stays lit | Then every monster strikes at the same time. |
| 0:28 | The result card: "First to find it", the way in words, then the hunt with first finders' avatars | This trial has three ways to win. Nobody had found this one, so it carries my name, and the subreddit hunts the rest. |
| 0:37 | Build a trial: place a wolf and a boar, Check, clear it to prove it, post it | Anyone can build a trial. The solver checks it can be won, you clear it to prove it, and it goes up for everyone. |
| 0:47 | Final shot: tap Leave a chalk mark, then the comment appears in the day's thread, with the nation sigil in the flair beside the name | One tap leaves my result in the day's thread, with my nation and streak as flair. Tomorrow there's a new one. |

The final shot is the result landing in Reddit itself, not a number on a card. Do not end on the result card.

Before recording: the r/Foefall post must be the real installed app (not a playtest), and the account recording it needs
a nation picked so the flair shows.
